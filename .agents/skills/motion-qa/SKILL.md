# Motion QA
Test composition metadata, frame rate, frame count, dimensions and codec. Inspect contact sheet and individual stills at scene cuts and transition boundaries. Detect clipped text, overlaps, blank frames, inconsistent type and weak final hold. Write findings and fixes to docs/QA-REPORT.md. State clearly what was not tested.
